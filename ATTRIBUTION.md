# Clearform attribution and provenance

Clearform is developed through human direction, selection, editing and quality
assurance, with code and content generation assistance from OpenAI Codex. This
is a record of the project's process; OpenAI is not presented as the project's
author, sponsor or licensor.

## Design references

The visual direction is informed by general principles in Apple's publicly
available Human Interface Guidelines and by aspects of the iOS 7-era visual
language, including clarity, restrained ornament, fine separators and tinted
actions. Clearform is an independent web implementation and is not affiliated
with or endorsed by Apple Inc.

No Apple logos, artwork, source code, templates, typefaces, SF Symbols or Apple
Design Resources are included in this repository. Apple, iOS and related names
and marks belong to their respective owner. The detailed reference-to-design
mapping is documented in [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md).

The design-review process referenced
[`dickwu/apple-design-skill`](https://github.com/dickwu/apple-design-skill) at
commit `904b0eedc7cc778152f545506075d5bb5219ce77`. No files from that repository
are redistributed with Clearform.
